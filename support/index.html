Getting started

  • Klera runs on iPad. Apple Pencil gives the best experience; a finger works too.
  • No account is needed. Signing in with Apple or Google keeps your boards and progress in sync across devices.
  • Write a problem on a board, then tap Hint, Mistake, Next Step, Explain, Solve or Prove.

Common problems

  • The AI buttons do nothing. Klera asks before it sends anything to its AI providers. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy → AI tutoring.
  • "No internet connection". Writing works offline; hints, solutions and the voice need a connection.
  • The tutor can't hear me. Allow the microphone and speech recognition in the iPad's Settings → Klera.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored under it. To keep your account and remove only your learning data, use Delete my learning data instead. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.
