From PDF to marked script.

Past papers are the most effective revision there is, and almost nobody uses them properly. Klera turns one into a paper you sit, then marks it the way an examiner would.

  1. Upload the paper

    Any past paper as a PDF.

  2. Questions are parsed

    Each question and its real mark allocation.

  3. Write it by hand

    On the same ink canvas as the whiteboard, timed if you want.

  4. Graded

    Against the official mark scheme.

  5. Corrected

    Marks placed on your own working.

The mark scheme wins. Every time.

The hard part of automated marking is not reading handwriting. It is stopping the model from being clever.

A language model asked to grade will work the answer out itself and mark you against its own version. When those disagree, a correct student gets zero. So when a mark scheme is supplied, Klera’s grader is told plainly that the scheme is correct by definition: do not re-derive it, do not substitute your own answer, and if your reasoning disagrees, the scheme wins.

Without a mark scheme it has to solve the paper first. That is a weaker mode, and Klera does not present it as the same thing.

Marks out of the real maximum

The total for each question comes from the paper itself, never invented. Blank answers are marked as not attempted without spending a call on them.

Where the marks went

Feedback tied to the line that cost the mark, not a general reminder to show your working.

The answer the scheme wanted

The model solution is always the mark scheme’s answer, never the grader’s own contradicting version.

Marked on your own paper.

Feedback you have to mentally re-attach to your working is feedback you will not use. Klera returns a corrected image of your own answer, with the marks placed against the line that went wrong, the way a teacher marks in the margin.

A clean signal, labelled honestly.

An exam question is the least ambiguous evidence a tutor can have: a correct answer, a real maximum and an official scheme. That makes exam results the most reliable input the learner profile could have.

To be straight about it: that connection is not built yet. The grading works today. Feeding its results into the profile is in progress, and until it ships, we do not claim it.
