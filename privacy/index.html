Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10.

Klera AI ("Klera", "we", "the app") is an AI study tool for handwriting math and physics on iPad. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible and to keep your learning data private to you.

Who this is for

Klera is intended for students aged 13 and over, teachers, and parents. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. If you live in a country where the age of digital consent is higher than your age (up to 16 in parts of the EU), please use Klera with a parent or guardian's permission.

What we collect

  • Account — when you sign in with Google (or Apple), we receive your name, email address, and a user identifier. We use these to identify your account and secure your data.
  • Learning data — your handwritten work, per-topic mastery estimates, and behavioral signals the app uses to personalize tutoring (e.g. how long you work before asking for help, pen-motion patterns such as speed and pressure, help usage, session summaries). It also includes the tutor's estimate, from what you say or type in the tutor chat, of whether you seem stuck, frustrated or bored, so it can slow down or explain another way. This is stored under your account and used only to adapt the tutoring to you; it is never used to diagnose anything.
  • Content you create — boards, problems, and (if you use the exam features) exam answers and results. To provide AI tutoring, images or text of your current work are sent to our AI providers (below) to generate hints, checks, and solutions, together with your study level and what Klera has learned about how you work (for example topics you find hard and how you like explanations) — never your name or email. If you use the voice tutor, spoken/typed messages are processed to produce a response.
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance diagnostics via Apple's MetricKit, stored on your device and never sent to us.
  • Abuse prevention — when the app asks Klera's server for AI help, the server sees your IP address and a random installation identifier (different from the analytics one) and keeps them for up to 48 hours to enforce fair-use limits. They are not linked to your account.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — anonymous feature-usage events (for example "a hint was requested", how long the app was open, how much AI processing a session used) that help us improve Klera. Each event also carries the app and iPadOS version and the country of your App Store account (country only — never your precise location or IP address), and the study level and favourite subjects you chose (for example "university, 2nd year, engineering"). These use a random installation identifier, are never linked to your account, name, or email, and never include your handwriting, problem content, or school. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage analytics; deleting your data also resets the identifier and stops collection. We do not use advertising SDKs and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

To provide and personalize the tutoring, sync your data across your devices, keep your account secure, and fix crashes. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it (service providers)

We use these processors solely to run the service; they process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted storage of your account and learning data.
  • Google / Apple — sign-in (identity verification).
  • OpenAI — generating tutoring (hints, mistake checks, solutions) from images/text of your work.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech for the voice tutor.
  • Apple — speech recognition: when you talk to the tutor, your speech is converted to text on the iPad whenever it supports that; on older iPads Apple's speech recognition service does it.
  • PostHog (EU-hosted) — anonymous usage analytics only (see above); never receives your identity or content.

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Requests to OpenAI and ElevenLabs go through Klera's own server, which holds the API keys; the app never contacts them directly.

To recognise what kind of problem you wrote (for example a proof), Klera reads your handwriting on the iPad itself with Apple's Vision framework; that reading never leaves the device.

Your permission comes first. Before Klera sends anything to OpenAI or ElevenLabs for the first time, the app asks you, explaining what is sent and to whom. If you choose Not now, nothing is sent and the AI features stay off. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy → AI tutoring.

Your data is yours (rights)

  • Export — Settings → Account → Export my learning data downloads a JSON copy of your learning data (no third-party content).
  • Delete your learning data — Settings → Account → Delete my learning data permanently removes your learning profile from this device and our cloud, and keeps your account.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything stored under it: your profile, classes you teach (with their handouts and assignments), exams you created, your exam results and class memberships, and your learning profile and history. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Klera's access to your Apple ID. This cannot be undone. Boards saved on your iPad are not uploaded to us, so they stay on the iPad until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Signing out instead reverts the app to a local, anonymous state.
  • Access / questions — contact us (below). Depending on your location you may have additional rights under your local data-protection law, such as the GDPR.

Data security

Access tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Your learning profile is protected by row-level security so only your signed-in account can read or write it. Data in transit is encrypted.

Retention

We keep your learning data while your account is active. When you delete it (above) it is removed from our cloud, and your learning profile is removed from the device. Boards on the device stay until you delete them or uninstall the app.

We also apply automatic limits so nothing is kept indefinitely by default:

  • Detailed working records — the per-problem behavioural detail (pen pressure, writing speed, pauses and similar signals) is removed after 12 months without activity. Your topic progress is kept, so the tutor still knows you if you come back.
  • Everything else — a learning profile untouched for 24 months is deleted in full.
  • On the device — the app keeps only the last 40 problem records and 7 days of daily activity; older detail is discarded as you use it, not archived.

Newsletter and early-access list (website)

If you subscribe to the newsletter on our website, we store your email, the role you picked (student, parent, teacher or other), your consent and the date. We use it only to send you the Klera newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you need to be 16 or older, or have a parent's permission, to subscribe.

Before the app launched, the same list collected early-access sign-ups: your email, and optionally your name, role, university or school, device, what you study and an Apple ID email for TestFlight, plus a parent or guardian email for under-18s. We keep those entries to honour the free year of Premium promised to testers, and we only email them about Klera.

Both are stored with Supabase and are not readable from the website. They are never sold or shared for marketing. To be removed, unsubscribe from any email or write to filip@klera.tech and we will delete your entry.

Website visits

When you visit klera.tech we count page visits with PostHog (EU-hosted): the page, the referring site, your browser, device type and country. Your IP address is not stored, no cookies or other identifiers are saved on your device, and visits are never linked to you or to the app.

Children & schools

Where the app is used through a school, the school may act as the data controller for student data; we act as a processor on the school's behalf. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

Changes

We will update this policy as the app evolves and post the new version at this URL with a revised date.
