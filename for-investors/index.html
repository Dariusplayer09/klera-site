The oldest result in education

One-to-one tutoring beats every other intervention we know of.

In 1984 Benjamin Bloom reported that students taught one to one performed around two standard deviations better than students taught in a class: the average tutored student outperformed nearly everyone in the classroom. Forty years of follow-up research has put the size of the effect lower, but tutoring remains one of the largest, most reliably replicated effects in education.

Bloom called it the two sigma problem: not how to make tutoring work, but how to give its benefit to everyone when a human tutor for every student is unaffordable.

What a tutor actually does

It is not the explanations. Those are in every textbook. It is watching the student work: seeing the hesitation before the first line, the erasing, the moment they go quiet, and stepping in with exactly the right amount of help. Then stepping back.

Answer machines make it worse

The fastest way to satisfy a student is the surest way to stop them learning.

The evidence

In a large field experiment (Bastani et al., 2024), students given an unrestricted chatbot did better on practice problems and worse on the exam once it was taken away. A version designed to guide rather than answer removed most of that harm.

Blind between messages

A chat window sees a student twice: when the question is typed and when the next one arrives. Everything a tutor reacts to happens in between, and is lost.

The opening

The product that wins is the one that makes students better, not the one that makes homework faster. That needs a different kind of input, and a tutor willing to hold back.

Why it's hard to copy

Three different kinds of difficulty, stacked.

Data

Signal nobody else collects

How a student writes, pauses, erases and asks for help only exists on a native canvas with a pressure-sensitive pen. A chat or web product cannot get it by trying harder.

Craft

Teaching, not answering

Knowing when to hold back, and writing back as natural handwriting on the student's own page, took four generations of engine to get right. It is the part a general AI company has no reason to build.

Compounding

It gets better the longer you stay

Every session sharpens the model of that student. Two months in, the help is calibrated to them. Leaving means starting from a blank page.
