Why it works

A chatbot sees a student twice: when the question is typed, and when the next one is. Everything a real tutor watches for happens in between.

Data nobody else collects

Pauses, erasures, pressure, speed, and how a student asks for help. It only exists on a native canvas with a pressure-sensitive pen. A chat or web product cannot get it by trying harder.

It knows when not to answer

When a student is ready, Klera writes one step and makes them produce the next line, watching the page until they do. Handing over the answer feels good and teaches nothing.

It gets better the longer you stay

The profile takes weeks of real work to build, and Klera scores every teaching choice against what the student did next. Leaving means starting again from a blank page.
