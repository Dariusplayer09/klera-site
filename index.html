What it actually is

An iPad whiteboard

You write a problem with your Apple Pencil, the way you would on paper. Nothing to type, nothing to photograph, no chat box.

A tutor that writes back

Klera answers in pen strokes on your own canvas, next to your working, and talks you through it. Not a wall of text beside the page.

A read on how you are doing

Pressure, speed, hesitation before a stroke, erase bursts. Measured against your own normal, not a population average, and never shown to you as a score.
