About us

The tutor we wished we'd had.

Between us we've studied in Polish, American, Swiss IB, Romanian and British schools, and now we're both at an Italian university. We saw how much the system you're born into decides how well you get taught.

Why we started Klera

We're the first students to study with ChatGPT and other AIs from day one. They're brilliant at giving answers. But we started to notice we were depending on them instead of learning. The homework got done, and the understanding didn't come with it.

A good tutor works the other way round. They sit next to you, watch you work, make you try first, and step in only when you need it. They learn how you think, and they teach you the way that works for you.

That's what Klera is: a personal, adaptive tutor that learns how you learn and adjusts to you. It lets you answer the questions yourself and makes you try first. It all happens with a real pen on a real page, like a tutor sitting right next to you.
