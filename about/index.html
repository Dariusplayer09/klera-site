About

We wanted a tutor that could see the page.

Klera started from a small, annoying observation: every AI that helps with maths makes you leave the page to ask it something, and then answers somewhere that is not the page. The thing a real tutor does, sitting next to you, watching the pen, was missing from all of them.

So we built the version that sits at the desk. It watches how you work, it writes back in handwriting on your own canvas, and it makes you produce the next line yourself when it judges you can.

How we work

We show the product, never an imitation of it

Every image and clip of Klera on this site is a real capture from the app. We do not draw handwriting in code to illustrate a handwriting engine, and we do not ship a recording of an older engine because it is the one we happen to have.

We separate what we measured from what we model

The engineering page publishes real per-attempt tables from instrumented device runs, including the sessions where our own estimator was talking over the student. Anything modelled rather than measured says so on the figure itself.

We do not score the student

The model of a student is detailed and it is never shown to them. No percentage, no streak, no progress bar. Being told you are at 71 percent does not help anyone do the next question, and it makes people work for the number instead of the maths.
