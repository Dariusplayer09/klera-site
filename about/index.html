The tutor we wished we'd had.

Every AI that helps with maths makes you leave the page to ask it something, then answers somewhere that is not the page. The thing a real tutor does, sitting next to you and watching the pen, was missing from all of them.

We study for handwritten STEM exams every semester. We are the users.

The team

Two Bocconi students building the tutor we wished we'd had.

How we work

We show the product, never an imitation

Every screenshot and clip of Klera on this site is a real capture from the app. We never draw handwriting in code to illustrate a handwriting engine.

We separate measured from modelled

Anything we publish as a measurement came from a real session on a real iPad. Anything modelled says so.

We don't score the student

The model of a student is detailed, and it is never shown to them. No percentage, no streak, no progress bar.
