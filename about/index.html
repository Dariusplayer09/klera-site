About

We wanted a tutor that could see the page.

Klera started from a small, annoying observation: every AI that helps with maths makes you leave the page to ask it something, and then answers somewhere that is not the page. The bit a real tutor does, sitting next to you, watching the pen, was missing from all of them.

How we work

We show the product, never an imitation of it

Every image of Klera on this site is a real capture from the app. We do not draw handwriting in code to illustrate a handwriting engine, and where a real shot does not exist yet there is a visible gap instead of a mock-up.

We publish the runs that went badly

The engineering page includes the sessions where our own estimator was talking over the student, the reading that invented a struggling student out of queue depth, and the signal we cannot currently fire. Those are the measurements that tell you whether the rest is real.

We do not score the student

The model of a student is detailed and it is never shown to them. No percentage, no streak, no progress bar. Being told you are at 71 percent does not help anyone do the next question.
