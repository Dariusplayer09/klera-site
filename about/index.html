Built to be needed less.

Tutoring is paid by the hour, so it quietly profits from being needed more. We built the opposite.

Thinking looks exactly like doing nothing.

Get that wrong and a tutor interrupts someone at the moment they were about to get it.

That single problem explains most of what is unusual about Klera: why four of the five help actions refuse to give the answer, why tapping Solve counts against independence, and why so much of the system exists to tell a student who is stuck from one who is simply thinking.

What we decided early and kept.

Silence over a confident guess

When there is not enough evidence, the system reports nothing. A zero looks like a measurement. Nothing looks like nothing, which is the truth.

Never invent a student

Anything the tutor is told about a learner comes from that learner’s own recorded work, or it is left out.

Support only adds

Evidence of difficulty can add help. Nothing inferred can take it away.

We wrote down the mistake

An early version shipped placeholder text claiming every student “tends to drop negative signs”, and it reached live prompts. It is the mistake we guard against hardest, and it is why the rule above exists.

Measure before steering

When we added a way to tell whether a visualisation helped, we did not let it change which one is shown. Change behaviour first and the baseline that would judge the change is gone.

The board belongs to the student

Help arrives as a line of handwriting where the line belongs, and only when asked for. No assistant hovering over your working.

What we were looking at.

A good one-to-one tutor. They do not know more maths. They watch, and they step in at the right moment with the smallest nudge that works. Klera tries to reproduce that behaviour, not the lecturing.

Paper. Real working is messy, spatial and crossed out, and all of that mess is signal. So the surface had to be ink, and the tutor had to answer in ink.

Instruments. A good instrument reports what it measured and is honest about its uncertainty. The rule that a reading below the reliability gate shows nothing at all came from there.

Two of us.

Klera is built by two people, who are also, for now, its only two measured students. That is why the learner profile page can say exactly how each of us struggles, and why it is careful to say two people is not a study.
